Since the end of the Perl Toolchain Summit in Vienna, at the end of
April, the organizers have been busy wrapping up 2026, and preparing for
2027. One of our main goals for this year is to be ready long before
2027 has even started.

And so today we're happy to announce that the 17th Perl Toolchain
Summit will be held in Lyon, France, from Thursday April 29 till Sunday
May 2, 2027.

This post is brought to you by [The Perl and Raku Foundation](https://perlfoundation.org/),
a recurring Diamond sponsor of the Perl Toolchain Summit.

![The Perl & Raku Foundation logo](/images/announcing-the-perl-toolchain-summit-2027/logo_tprf.png)

---

Started in 2008 as the Perl QA Hackathon in Oslo, the Perl Toolchain
Summit is an annual event that brings together the key developers
working on the Perl toolchain. Each year (except for 2020-2022), the
event moves from country to country all over Europe, organized by local
teams of volunteers. The surplus money from previous summits helps fund
the next one.

Since 2023, the organizing team is formally split between a "global"
team and a "local" team (although this setup has been informally
used before).

Our global team is made up of veteran PTS organizers, who deal with
invitations, finding sponsors, paying bills and communications. They are
Laurent Boivin ([ELBEHO](https://metacpan.org/author/ELBEHO)), Philippe
Bruhat ([BOOK](https://metacpan.org/author/BOOK)), Thibault Duponchelle
([CONTRA](https://metacpan.org/author/CONTRA)), and Breno de Oliveira
([GARU](https://metacpan.org/author/GARU)), supported by [Les Mongueurs
de Perl](https://www.mongueurs.net/)'s bank account.

This year, the local team is just Philippe, who lives near Lyon.
This will be the fourth Perl Toolchain Summit held in the city,
meaning we already have all the local stuff very well in hand.

We'll return to our usual venue there, a hotel in front of the train
station and across the street from the airport shuttle, which has hosted
the Summit in 2017 and 2023 (and one Sunday in 2014).

The developers who maintain CPAN and associated tools and services are
all volunteers, scattered across the globe. This event is the one time
in the year when they can get together.

The summit provides dedicated time to work on the critical systems
and tools, with all the right people in the same room. The attendees
hammer out solutions to thorny problems and discuss new ideas to keep
the toolchain moving forward. We're still working on the attendees list
at the moment, and we expect 30 to 35 people to join us in Lyon next year.

Given the important nature of the attendees' work and their volunteer
status, we try to pay for most expenses (travel, lodging, food, etc.)
through sponsorship. Another goal for this year is to break even (we've
been running the summit at a loss for the past few years, using up our
treasure chest). If you're interested in helping sponsor the summit,
please get in touch with the global team at
<pts2027@perltoolchainsummit.org>.

[The Perl and Raku Foundation](https://perlfoundation.org/) has been
sponsoring the Perl Toolchain Summit for several years now, either via
direct financial contributions, or by funding travel for some of our
attendees. We are very grateful for their continued support.

> The Perl and Raku Foundation is delighted to once again support the
> Perl Toolchain Summit as a Diamond Sponsor. The Summit brings together
> the dedicated people who build and maintain the tools and infrastructure
> the Perl community relies on every day. TPRF is proud to help give these
> contributors the time and space to work together, solve problems, and
> strengthen the ecosystem for everyone. The Foundation looks forward to
> another productive and inspiring Perl Toolchain Summit in 2027.
